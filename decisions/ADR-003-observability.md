# ADR-003 — Central Observability

Status: Accepted

Important events from Odoo, n8n, Slack, WooCommerce, Gmail, Drive, AI and future integrations should be centrally searchable/correlated.

The system should answer:
- who did it?
- what bot/service did it?
- when?
- where did it originate?
- what changed?
- did it succeed?
- why did it fail?
- which AI model/cost was involved?
